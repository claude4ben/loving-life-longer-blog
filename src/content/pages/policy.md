It is **not** medical, psychological, nutritional, fitness, financial or legal advice. It isn't meant to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.

- **Talk to a professional first.** Check with your GP or another qualified professional before you change your diet, exercise, sleep, medication or mental-health care. Do this especially if you're pregnant, have a health condition or take medication.
- **Don't delay or ignore professional advice** because of something you read here.
- **Try things at your own risk.** Activities written about here, such as cold water, fasting, breathwork, intense exercise, retreats and festivals, carry real risks. They aren't right for everyone. What worked for Ben may not work for you.

- **In an emergency, get help now.** In the UK, call **999**, or **111** for urgent medical advice. If you're struggling, you can talk to **Samaritans** any time, free, on **116 123**.

Links to other websites are shared in good faith. We don't control those sites, and a link doesn't mean we endorse everything on them.

## Comments policy

Comments are for sharing experiences, asking questions and supporting each other. To keep this a good place to be:

**Before you post**

- Your **name and comment are public** and may appear in search engines. Use a name you're happy to show; a first name or nickname is fine.
- Your **email is optional** and never published. If you give it, we only use it to let you know we have replied.
- You need to be **13 or older** to comment.

**House rules**

- **Be kind and respectful.** No insults, harassment, bullying, threats or hate based on who someone is.
- **Stay on topic** and keep it constructive. Disagreeing is fine; attacking people isn't.
- **No medical claims or selling.** Please don't promise cures, give diagnoses, or promote products, services or treatments.
- **No spam, adverts or self-promotion.** That includes affiliate or referral links.
- **Protect privacy.** Don't share personal details (yours or anyone else's), such as addresses, phone numbers or health information about other people.
- **Keep it legal.** Nothing unlawful, defamatory, obscene, or that you don't have the right to share.

**Moderation**

- We read comments and may hold, edit (for example, to remove personal details) or delete any comment that breaks these rules, without notice. We may block people who repeatedly break them.
- Some comments may need approval before they appear.
- Publishing a comment doesn't mean we agree with it. Comments are the views of the people who wrote them.

**Your comment, your words**

You keep ownership of what you write. By posting, you let Loving Life Longer show it on this site, and quote it with your display name in LLL content such as the newsletter or podcast. You're responsible for what you post.

**Reporting a comment or deleting your own**

Email us at the address in the Contact section with a link to the comment. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

## Privacy and your data

This section explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). The **data controller** is Ben Parker, trading as Loving Life Longer.

**What we collect and why**

- **Comments:** your name, comment and the date. *Why:* to show your comment and run the discussion. *Legal reason:* legitimate interests (running a community).
- **Email address on a comment** (optional): only to let you know when we've replied. *Legal reason:* legitimate interests; you choose whether to give it.
- **Newsletter email** (when the newsletter launches): to send the emails you asked for. *Legal reason:* your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **Likes:** a random code stored in your browser, created only when you tap Like, so each person's like counts once. It isn't linked to who you are. *Legal reason:* legitimate interests.
- **Security records:** a scrambled (one-way hashed) version of the IP address of anyone who fails to sign in to the admin area, to block password guessing. Deleted within a day. *Legal reason:* legitimate interests (keeping the site secure).

We **don't** use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. We don't sell your data or use it for marketing you didn't ask for.

**Newsletter:** the email sign-up box isn't switched on yet. Addresses typed into it are **not** stored. When the newsletter launches, we'll update this page before collecting any addresses.

**Who else handles data for us**

- **Cloudflare** hosts the site and its database, and protects it from attacks. Like any web host, it processes technical information such as your IP address so the site can work.
- When the newsletter and reply emails start, we'll name the email service here.

Cloudflare (and, later, the email service) may process data outside the UK. When they do, they use the legal safeguards UK law requires, such as UK adequacy regulations or the UK's approved data-transfer contracts.

**How long we keep it**

- **Comments and any email address on them:** while the article is online, or until you ask us to delete them.
- **Likes:** while the article is online. You can remove your like by tapping the heart again. Clearing your browser's storage removes the code from your device.
- **Security records:** deleted within 24 hours.

**Your rights**

You can ask us to:

- see the personal information we hold about you
- correct it
- delete it
- restrict or object to how we use it
- give you a copy in a common format

You can also withdraw consent at any time. Email us (see Contact) and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the UK regulator, the **Information Commissioner's Office**, at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) or on 0303 123 1113.

**Children:** this site isn't aimed at children. Please don't comment or sign up if you're under 13.

## Cookies and local storage

We keep this to the minimum the site needs to work, so **you won't see a cookie banner**.

- **Visitors:** we don't set any advertising, tracking or analytics cookies.
- **Likes:** if you tap Like, a random code is saved in your browser's local storage, so your like counts once and is remembered. Nothing is saved if you only read.
- **Site owner only:** signing in to the admin area or the article editor sets cookies that keep the site owner signed in. Visitors never get these.
- **Security:** our host, Cloudflare, may set a strictly necessary cookie to tell real visitors from harmful bots.

If we ever add analytics or anything else that needs your permission, we'll update this page and ask first.
